# Communication Rules

## Allowed Communication

- Roles communicate only through edges defined in the role graph.
- Upward communication (escalation) follows the hierarchy chain.
- Cross-department communication goes through designated liaison edges.

## Prohibited Communication

- No direct communication between roles without an established edge.
- No bypassing the chain of command.
- No sharing context with roles outside the allowed graph.

## Human Communication

- Human stakeholders communicate only through CEO, CTO, or Product Manager.
- Other roles must escalate to one of these three to reach the human.

## Principles

- Do not guess requirements. Ask upward in the hierarchy when blocked.
- Save important decisions as context artifacts.
- Use only approved context when executing.
- Do not silently overwrite context.
- Do not bypass communication permissions.
